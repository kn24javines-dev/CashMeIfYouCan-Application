About the Project

Cash Me If You Can is a DFA-assisted payment verification prototype for small merchants accepting GCash payments. It checks reference number format and helps spot duplicate or mismatched entries before releasing orders.

How to Use

  1. Go to the Home tab and fill in the order and GCash payment details.
  2. Toggle the DFA State Diagram space on or off on the right as needed.
  3. Click VERIFY PAYMENT to check the entry (backend logic coming next).
  4. Open the Database tab to view recorded transactions.
